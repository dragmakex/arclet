# Demo script

Target length: about three minutes, narrated by a human.

1. State that Arclet is on Arc Testnet and test tokens have no monetary value.
2. Show Privy email login and the embedded personal wallet. Show a real USDC transfer receipt into the distinct Circle trading wallet and explain the custody change.
3. Enter a tiny supported strategy. Review market mapping, Graph source, amount, reserve, daily cap, slippage, execution count, and expiry. Sign the exact mandate.
4. Show the live Graph deployment, source chain, pool, block and observation. Let the normal worker evaluate and submit through Circle without a second trade click.
5. Show a confirmed Arc receipt and reconciled input/output. Then show a real HOLD caused by a required guard.
6. Pause without the model and explain that an already-submitted transaction may still settle. Show the authorized return transfer if time permits.
7. Close with the application-custody, proxy/cross-network, testnet, and mainnet limitations.

Do not record until the live artifacts exist. No AI narration or fabricated sequence.
